Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Quiet is a student prototype: a calm planner that turns a “brain dump” into a realistic plan and reminds you to look after yourself. It is built so that your data stays with you. There is no Quiet database and no Quiet account; the only server part is a small relay for the shared AI, described below, which keeps nothing.

What Quiet stores, and where

  • On your device. Everything you add — tasks, events, notes, goals, habits, energy check-ins, focus sessions and “parked” thoughts — is saved in your browser’s or app’s local storage on that device.
  • In your own Google Drive (only if you connect Google). To keep your devices in sync, Quiet saves one file in a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive (the appDataFolder). Quiet cannot see any of your other Drive files, and nobody else can see this one.

What Quiet does with your Google account

When you choose Connect Google, Quiet asks for:

  • Your name, email and profile picture — to show which account is connected and to greet you by name.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata) — to read and write the single sync file described above.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events) — to read your events so meetings and classes count as busy time when Quiet plans your day, and to add, update or remove the events Quiet creates for you: exams, meetings, hard deadlines and tasks with a set time. Quiet never edits or deletes events it didn’t create.

This information is used only to provide these features to you. Quiet’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never sent to the people who made Quiet.

AI features

When you use the brain dump, “Break it into steps” or the weekly reflection, the text involved — and any file or photo you attach to a brain dump — is sent to an AI model to be sorted or summarised:

  • Quiet AI (the default, shared): the request passes through a small Quiet server function on Netlify to Groq, which runs the AI model. The function only forwards the request and returns the answer; it does not store or log what you send. Groq’s own privacy terms apply.
  • Your own key (optional): if you add a key for Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, requests go directly from your device to that provider instead, under its terms. Your key is stored on your devices and, if you connect Google, in your Drive sync file.
  • No AI: you can turn AI off in You → AI; a built-in sorter then runs entirely on your device.

No tracking

Quiet has no analytics, no ads, no cookies for tracking and no third-party trackers. The app is hosted on Netlify, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is loaded.

Deleting your data

  • In Quiet, go to You → Your data → Erase this device.
  • Go to You → Google → Disconnect to revoke Quiet’s access. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the sync file, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Quiet → Delete hidden app data.
  • Events Quiet added to Google Calendar can be deleted there like any other event.

User studies

If you take part in a study of Quiet, the optional study mode records how well Quiet sorted your brain dumps, only on your device. Nothing is shared unless you choose to export it and hand it over.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to the support email shown on Google’s sign-in screen for Quiet.
